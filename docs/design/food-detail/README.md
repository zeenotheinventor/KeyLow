## Decisions

Where the sources disagree, the order is: Zee's rulings, then the Ki-Lo design system (`../design-system/`), then MacroFactor for layout and behaviour, then the briefs. Zee approved these boards on 2026-10-13. Anything that would visibly change a board goes back to Zee first.

- **Detail opens inside the search sheet.** Back returns to the previous view in the sheet and close dismisses the whole sheet. There is no tab bar and no "log this food" action in M2.
- **Hero card with "% of energy" tiles.** Energy is the large numeral, with protein, carbohydrate and fat beside it. "% of energy" is Atwater 4/4/9 over the stated energy (protein x 4, carbohydrate x 4, fat x 9, divided by kcal), rounded to a whole per cent. The shares need not sum to 100, because fibre and alcohol are not counted, which is why the caption calls them estimates.
- **Values are per 100 g or per 100 ml, and the basis is always written.** A missing value reads "not given" in a quieter tone and is never drawn as 0. A missing share shows no pill.
- **Missing values.** A shared food with missing values shows the "Needs review" Banner (warning tone, never danger) with the single action "Copy to your foods". This replaces the old "Review and save as my food". The Banner body is written for M2, since the system's default body talks about logging.
- **Source names are plain:** UK CoFID, Open Food Facts and Yours. Attribution appears on shared foods only, never on your own foods.
- **Own foods** get an Edit action. Shared foods are never changed.
- **Edit and copy loading, load error and not-found reuse `loading`, `error` and `not-found`** (orchestrator call, 2026-10-13). The food form has no boards of its own for those states.
- **`quantity-proposal-m3` is not M2 scope.** MacroFactor pins a quantity field, unit chips and "Add" to detail. That needs logging, so it is drawn as an M3 proposal only.
- **Not shown on purpose:** the barcode, saturated fat, sugars, salt and micronutrients, and a converted-from-kilojoules label (still a question for Zee).

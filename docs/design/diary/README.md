## Decisions

Where the sources disagree, the order is: Zee's rulings, then the Ki-Lo design system (`../design-system/`), then MacroFactor for layout and behaviour, then the briefs. These boards are on the canvas "Ki-Lo M3 diary screens" and are **not yet approved by Zee** ([KEY-178](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-178)). Only items tagged Q1 to Q9 are Zee's rulings ([KEY-62](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-62), 2026-10-15). Everything else is brief or canvas content, and items marked as a recommendation are the orchestrator's, all awaiting Zee's approval on KEY-178.

- **Four meal slots,** Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks, not MacroFactor's hourly timeline (Q1). `logged_at` is still stored, so a timeline can come later.
- **The hero is CalorieRing and MacroBars** (Q5). The ring is 200 across with a 44 px remaining numeral (smaller at Zee's request, 2026-10-15, and drawn that way on the canvas). The manual target shows without "(est.)" (recommendation).
- **Log rows show values for the amount logged** ("150 g · P 48 g · F 3.3 g · C 0 g"), not per 100 g.
- **The week shows as a chip and a sheet:** "n incomplete days this week" counts Monday to today, never future days. MacroFactor's week strip is `week-strip-proposal`, not built.
- **Delete is immediate with an Undo toast,** no confirm (Q7). The toast dismisses after 5 s (recommendation). Swipe has a non-gesture route: Delete on the entry screen.
- **Offline** shows the last saved diary from the persisted cache and disables the add actions, the slot options and the complete toggle (brief 10). Rows stay readable.
- `diary.today-scrolled` shows the lower half of the same day, so the build has the Dinner, Snacks and complete-toggle positions.
- **Numbers.** Calories are whole numbers with a thousands separator (2,200). Grams take one decimal only under 10. Over target is warning, never danger.

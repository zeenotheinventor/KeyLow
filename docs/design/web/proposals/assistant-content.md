# Web assistant content kinds

Boards for [KEY-992](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-992) (web assistant content kinds, part of [KEY-988](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-988), the web assistant). They draw the content the phone's assistant gained after the 26 web boards of 25 October, inside that frame: the docked panel at 1280 (400 px wide, under the top bar) and the full-screen sheet at 375, from canvas "Ki-Lo assistant screens". The design gate is met by these boards under Zee's MWEB ruling of 2026-11-05. Zee was away on 2026-11-12 and asked to follow the phone when in doubt, so every choice below is marked "Recommended (Zee away), reversible".

Each board is a PNG at device pixel ratio 2 and a page that opens from `file://`. Frames are 1280 dark, 1280 light, 375 dark and 375 light, left to right, one row per state. Pages link `packages/tokens` by relative path and use no raw hex.

The shell is the one merged today (`DashboardNav`): the mark, Dashboard, Diary, Train and More, the Search for a food launcher, then the Assistant button. The top bar is 65 px, so the panel starts at 65 px. Boards about food sit over the Diary on Sunday 25 October, the golden day of the 26 boards. Boards about training sit over the Train page, drawn as placeholders for the cards as built.

## Decisions

1. Recommended (Zee away), reversible: the working line's mark is `KiLoMark` extended with the canvas prototype's chosen loop, "Loading the bar" (`design-canvas-assistant-render/assistant/loading-mark-prototype.html`, `POSES.load`). The engineer samples `POSES.load` or writes its numbers as CSS keyframes. They are: one loop of 2200 ms, linear, started 0.72 of the way through so the first frame is the complete logo. `span(t, a, b)` is `t` scaled from `a` to `b` and clamped to 0 to 1. `inOut` is the cubic ease in and out. `backOut(x)` is `1 + (c + 1)(x - 1)^3 + c(x - 1)^2` with `c = 1.4`, a small overshoot. Plate travel is 52 units along the bar's axis (each unit moves `x` and `y` by `1/√2`), the first plate outward up and right, the second down and left. Plate one slides in over `span(t, 0.03, 0.2)` with `backOut` and fades in over 0.03 to 0.09. Plate two the same over 0.29 to 0.46, fading in over 0.29 to 0.35. Each arc is a `stroke-dasharray` of `length 1000` on the r 100 circle, full length 205, arc one turned -9 degrees and arc two 187.4 degrees (the dash pattern 205 122 repeats at 327 units). Arc one draws with `inOut` over 0.13 to 0.36, arc two over 0.39 to 0.62. From 0.82 to 0.98 both plates travel back out with `inOut` and fade out over 0.86 to 0.98, and both arcs erase from their start (the dash offset moves by the same 205 times `inOut`). As each plate lands the whole dumbbell (bar and plates) knocks across the bar, along the other diagonal, by `translate(move, move)` with `move` equal to the knock times `1/√2`, the knock being `7 · e^(-22u) · sin(46u)`, with `u` the time since 0.14 and since 0.4. An arc shorter than half a unit is hidden. The board draws the resting frame, the complete logo: bar, both plates and both arcs at 205. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the mark is that resting frame and does not move. The phone's worklet maths stays in the app (decision 5 of [KEY-988](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-988)). Colours: the ring in `--accent`, the bar and plates in `--text-primary`, as `KiLoMark` has them.
2. Recommended (Zee away), reversible: the table is one scroller, not the phone's two views. A wrapper holds the 1 px border, the medium radius and the fade, with `position: relative` and `overflow: hidden`. Inside it the scroller is `overflow-x: auto` and holds a grid of every cell. The first column's cells are `position: sticky; left: 0` in that same scroller, on `--surface-sheet`, at most 152 px with a 1 px line on their right, names clamped to two lines at 127 px. Other columns are `max-content` up to 200 px, numbers right aligned with tabular figures, and the grid makes every row as tall as its tallest cell. While the table overflows, the scroller has `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` and the name "Table, 7 rows." (`tableLabel`, the row count and `tableRowMany`), so it is reached by Tab and scrolls with the arrow keys, a trackpad, or Shift and the wheel. The kit focus ring is drawn on the wrapper through `:has(:focus-visible)`, because a ring on the scroller would be cut by the wrapper's rounded clip. The scrollbar is the browser's own (`scrollbar-width: thin`). While columns are left to see, the wrapper's right border turns transparent, the table runs into the reply's 16 px right padding, and a 24 px fade into `--surface-sheet` covers the edge: the fade is `aria-hidden` and `pointer-events: none`. Scrolled to the end, the border takes its colour back and the fade goes. A table that fits fills the reply, does not scroll and is not focusable, its first column taking the spare width. The 1280 panel and the 375 sheet both scroll the Christmas market table, since 7 columns are wider than either.
3. Recommended (Zee away), reversible: a table arriving keeps its next row back until it is complete, and the caret sits in the empty row under the last, in the first column, as the phone does. Send is off and the field keeps focus while the reply streams.
4. Recommended (Zee away), reversible: the muscles card in the thread is always 2D (web 3D stays parked), 96 px a figure, front and back side by side, with no Undo and no link, as `assistant-card · exercise-muscles` draws it. Two exercises are two cards, the newest at the bottom.

No player and no YouTube frame is ever loaded in Ki-Lo: the poster is the thumbnail image the phone shows. This follows the merged phone card, which opens the watch address and plays nothing in the app, and it meets "no player loaded until pressed". With no video, the card is the phone's `FormPhotos`: one 2:1 stage holding the start photo with its Start badge, and the end photo with its End badge fading in over it and out again, each photo's turn 1.5 seconds with the crossfade, in a loop. The board draws the stage at the start photo. Only under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` do the two photos sit side by side at 3:2 each, still, 2 px apart on `--border`, with Start and End, which the third row draws. Each photo is an image named by `describeFormPhoto` ("Barbell back squat, start position"). The loading, unavailable and image failed states are the `exercise-form · form-media-card` board's, at the panel's width.

11. Recommended (Zee away), reversible, as the phone does it (`ANNOUNCEMENT_GAP_MS` in `apps/mobile/components/AssistantSheet/WorkingLine/WorkingLine.tsx`): the working line is a gap-gated live region. ## The table and the screen reader

Coordinator's decision, following the phone (the `ReplyTable` doc comment says the table has no accessibility of its own): the table's cells are `aria-hidden`, and the reply message carries one accessible reading built by core's `replyWords` (`packages/core/src/assistant/replyText.ts`), as the phone's `ChatMessage` builds its label. Run on a four-row sample (Bratwurst roll, Mulled wine, Baileys, and a last row that holds only "Chocolate") it reads, in full, "Table, 4 rows. Bratwurst roll, Amount 1 roll (190 g), kcal 520. Mulled wine, Amount 200 ml, kcal 170. Baileys, Amount 25 ml, kcal no value. Chocolate.", one sentence a row, each cell after its heading, a `–` read as "no value". On the web the message element gets that string as its accessible name (or a visually hidden copy), and the visible paragraphs and the grid are `aria-hidden`. The hiding goes on the grid inside the scroller, not on the scroller, so the focusable region is never inside `aria-hidden`: focus on it reads its name, "Table, 7 rows.".

While a table streams, the label is built as the phone builds it: `replyWords(paragraph, { isPartial: true, isArriving: true })` for the last paragraph. That holds back the unfinished row, so the reading and the region's name count only the rows that have arrived ("Table, 3 rows." with a fourth row half written), and the caret is never read. The label is not a live region, so it is read when the person moves to the message, and the reply finishing replaces it with the full reading.

## Carried from the phone fixes

From [KEY-982](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-982) and [KEY-983](https://linear.app/sojisoft/issue/KEY-983):

- The right border stays 1 px in both states and only its colour changes (transparent while columns are left to see, `--border` at the end). A width change would resize the scroller and re-run the end check, which made the table flicker at its far right.
- A table once judged wide stays wide until its own width, its number of columns or its number of rows change. Wrapped names in the capped first column can measure narrower than the same names given the spare width, so without this a table can flip between the two layouts.

How the web judges wide: a `ResizeObserver` on the scroller and on the grid compares `scrollWidth > clientWidth` (with 1 px of slack). While that holds, or the shape rule above keeps the table wide, the scroller has `tabindex="0"` and `role="region"` with its name, the first column is sticky and capped, and the fade shows until the end. When it does not, both attributes are removed and the table fits. The end is `scrollLeft >= scrollWidth - clientWidth - 1`, read on `scroll` and again when the observer fires, because the end moves as longer rows arrive.

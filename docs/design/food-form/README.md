## Decisions

Where the sources disagree, the order is: Zee's rulings, then the Ki-Lo design system (`../design-system/`), then MacroFactor for layout and behaviour, then the briefs. Zee approved these boards on 2026-10-13. Anything that would visibly change a board goes back to Zee first.

- **One form, three uses:** create your own food, save a completed copy of a shared food, and edit one of your own foods. Every save produces or updates a food you own. Shared CoFID and Open Food Facts foods are never changed. All three open inside the search sheet.
- **Buttons:** "Create food" for a new food, "Save to your foods" for a copy and "Save changes" for an edit.
- **Basis** is a SegmentedControl (per 100 g or per 100 ml), and the nutrient suffixes follow it.
- **`new-from-scan` locks the barcode** that a not-found scan carried over. Removing or rescanning it is an M3 idea.
- **Validation errors sit next to the field they are about** and are announced. The form never prints a raw error message. A fixed sentence per error kind comes from `packages/core` copy.
- **`save-partial`** is the state where the food saved but its portions did not. It says so and tells the user to save again.
- **`copy-off-error`** is a copy of an Open Food Facts product that is missing a required nutrient. The error sits next to that field.
- **Edit and copy loading, load error and not-found reuse the food detail boards** (orchestrator call, 2026-10-13), as the brief says. There are no separate form boards for them.
- **Out of scope for M2:** saturated fat, sugars, salt, micronutrients, density, photos, deleting a food, a Serving basis, a kJ switch and label scanning.

Badge art: tiers and series

Every badge is a struck medal: a metal frame, a dark enamel centre and a motif in the metal's colour. The frame climbs with the tier, the motif names the series, and a ribbon carries the threshold. The full set of 130 is on the next board.

One series, four tiers

The daily streak keeps its flame from start to finish. The frame is what climbs: Bronze is a plain struck ring, Silver adds a beaded rim, Gold sits on a sunburst with a shine, Platinum becomes a faceted gem with stars. The motif takes the colour of its metal.
